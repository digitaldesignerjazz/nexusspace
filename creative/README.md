# Creative & Immersive Layer

Narrative, roleplay, music, and immersive scenario work that both expresses the NexusSpace vision and serves as a testbed for agent memory, personality, long-context coherence, and human–AI co-creation.

## Subdirectories

- `roleplay/` — Long-form immersive roleplay sessions and character/world development
- `stories/` — Standalone or serialized stories and narrative experiments
- `music-suno/` — Suno AI (and other) music compositions tied to the nexus mythology
- `immersive-scenarios/` — Complex, multi-agent, or interactive scenario frameworks

Creative output here often directly informs technical work in `ai-agents/` (memory, emotional models, swarm coordination for storytelling).
