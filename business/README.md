# Business, Corporate & Governance

This directory holds corporate structure documentation, press materials, governance notes, and strategic planning for Esslinger & Co. and related entities that support the NexusSpace mission.

## Subdirectories

- `corporate/` — Corporate formation documents, share structures, board/governance models (high-level)
- `press-releases/` — External-facing announcements and narrative updates

Keep sensitive legal and financial details appropriately protected (use .gitignore or private repos for raw documents).
